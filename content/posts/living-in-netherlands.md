Moving to the Netherlands teaches you a few things very quickly.

## The Bikes

You will get a bike. Not because you want to, but because the infrastructure leaves you no choice. And honestly? It's one of the best things about living here.

## The Weather

It rains. A lot. But you stop caring after a while. You just become someone who owns three different jackets for three different types of rain.

## The Directness

Dutch people say what they mean. No sugarcoating. At first it feels rude. Then you realize how refreshing it is compared to passive-aggressive politeness.

## Gezelligheid

There's no English translation for *gezellig*. It's somewhere between cozy, fun, and warm social vibes. Once you understand it, you see it everywhere.

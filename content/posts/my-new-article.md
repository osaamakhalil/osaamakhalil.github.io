  Today I created this blog to test the ability of AI to do tasks like this.
Two years ago, if I wanted to build something like this, I would have had to read documentation, watch tutorials, spend time deciding which tools to use -- maybe even ask some friends like Mohamed Hamdy for advice. But today? I did it in **two prompts**. That's it.
Super powerful.
## The Game Has Changed
The game is no longer about your ability to *create* something. The game is about **what** that something will be.
Your thoughts -- are they valuable? If I start reading them, will I end up motivated at the gym? Or ordering a huge pizza? Or will I actually go learn something new?
**Your content is the product.**
## But That's Not Enough
Watch out -- even if you have an amazing product but you can't market it, then as the Dutch people say: *gefeliciteerd* (congratulations... to no one).
That's assuming you're creating content with some expectations. But if you're creating it just to share with family, friends, or whoever -- that's also great. And your mission is much easier.

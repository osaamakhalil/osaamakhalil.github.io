Everyone is chasing the next tool. The next framework. The next AI model.

But the real bottleneck was never the tool -- it was always the thinking behind it.

## The Tool Trap

We spend hours comparing tools, reading reviews, watching tutorials. By the time we pick one, we've already burned through our creative energy.

The best creators I know pick a tool and **stick with it**. They focus on the craft, not the instrument.

## What Actually Matters

- **Clarity of thought** -- Do you know what you're trying to say?
- **Consistency** -- Are you showing up regularly?
- **Feedback loops** -- Are you learning from what you ship?

The tool is just the vehicle. You're the driver.

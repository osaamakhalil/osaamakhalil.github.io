This is my first post on **Some Thoughts**. I built this blog using Hugo and GitHub Pages, and it deploys automatically whenever I push a new article.

> "The scariest moment is always just before you start." -- Stephen King

## Why I Started Writing

I've always believed that writing is thinking made visible. By putting my thoughts into words, I process ideas more deeply and hopefully share something useful along the way.

## How This Blog Works

The entire workflow is designed to be as frictionless as possible:

1. **Write** an article in Markdown
2. **Commit** and push to GitHub
3. **Done** -- the site rebuilds and deploys automatically

No databases. No CMS. No servers to manage. Just words and a git push.
